SMILE UP CLINIC — WEBSITE DEMO
=================================
Preview: open index.html in Chrome.

Included:
- Responsive one-page clinic website
- Doctor and clinic imagery cropped from the screenshots supplied in this chat
- Call, WhatsApp, email, Facebook and Google Maps buttons
- Appointment form
- Email button (opens the visitor's email app with a pre-filled appointment)
- WhatsApp button (opens a pre-filled message; visitor presses Send)

IMPORTANT BEFORE REAL DEPLOYMENT
- Automatic email delivery is NOT configured by the static demo. The email button uses mailto, so the visitor's email app must be configured. For automatic delivery, connect a form/email service and verify the clinic email.
- Confirm all credentials, service descriptions, timings and address with the clinic before publishing.
- Do not ask patients to enter sensitive medical information in this simple form.
- Replace the cropped images with original high-resolution clinic/doctor files if the clinic provides them.
